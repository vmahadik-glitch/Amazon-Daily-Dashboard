ℹ️ How to read this page▶

Everything here is Zeotap branded spend in this market — the revenue your team can influence by recommending segments.

Trajectory badges (click any for a full spend chart): Accelerating rising 15%+ day-over-day · Steady broadly flat · Decaying falling 15%+.

Segment chip: how many distinct segments an advertiser uses. Amber = 2 or fewer, a prime upsell opening.

Efficiency (expand a row): effective CPM vs. that advertiser's own median — efficient delivers cheaply, pricey costs more per impression.

🛑 Stopped = active 2+ of the last 3 days then zero. 🌱 New = started mid-window. 🎯 Upsell targets ranked High/Medium/Low by revenue at stake.

Note: with only a few days of data, day-over-day percentages are twitchy — treat single-day swings as a glance, not gospel. They stabilise as the month fills in.
